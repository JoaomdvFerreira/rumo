# MVP scope

The frozen MVP wedge is moving home / setting up a new home in Portugal, with deeper municipal support for Évora.

Eventual launch journeys: **Mudar de casa**, **Eletricidade e gás na nova casa**, and **Internet numa mudança**.

Deferred: Cartão Município Solidário, new life-event families, accounts, cross-device sync, database, AI routing, document upload, and automated submissions.
